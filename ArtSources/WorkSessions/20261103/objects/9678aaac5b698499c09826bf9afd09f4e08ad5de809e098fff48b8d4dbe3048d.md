# Fabius Normal source audit

**READY_FOR_COORDINATOR — pacote técnico portátil de 16 folhas/72 clips apenas.** A auditoria inicial de fonte continua aplicável ao pacote completo; este status significa que o subconjunto Parry/Hurt/Dead está empacotado e passou as verificações estáticas abaixo. Não é status de importação, runtime ou fechamento de Fabius inteiro. Scope: Fabius Normal only; no Unity, Git changes, or writes to project `Assets`/`ArtSources`.

- [Pacote portable-essentials](portable-essentials/README.md) contém exatamente 16 folhas, 72 clips e 144 frames: Katana, Punhos e Moto em Parry, Hurt e Dead. Exclui Walks; Moto Walk foi rejeitada diretamente por causa das pernas longas.
- Os 16 PNGs nativos revisados foram copiados sem alteração para a árvore técnica do pacote; todos os `sourceSha256` técnicos conferem com o `imageSha256` original. `provenance.originalAuthoredSource` preserva caminho/hash/dimensões históricos e registra `rawSourceAvailable:false`, sem alegar recuperação do raw path.
- 13 folhas são cópias byte a byte. As três Hurt com retângulos geometricamente sobrepostos foram empacotadas em atlas derivados sem escala: o canvas antigo permanece intacto, e os 24 crops completos foram copiados em RGBA para retângulos separados. IDs, pivôs, PPU, frame PPU, timings, loop e `guardContact` não mudaram. Os 144 hashes RGBA de frame conferem.
- A validação estática espelha `DarklightWeaponRedesignImporter.VerifySheet` e o subconjunto `TimedStateAction` de `DarklightWeaponRedesignBank.ValidPage`; tudo passou. Os 37 inputs protegidos mantiveram SHA-256 idêntico. `PAGES.complete`, runtime e publicação seguem `false`.
- `guardContact` permanece em 8/8 Moto Parry pages; não foi encontrado consumidor em Runtime/Editor C# neste checkout. A classe dedicada `DarklightFormController` não existe; a lógica de forma já existe no partial `DarklightCampaignTransformations.cs`.
- O gerador está no pacote e pode ser rerodado fora do projeto: `python prepare_portable_essentials.py --root <checkout> --out .`. Recusa output dentro do checkout e só grava nesse output. A execução pelo gerador externo e pela cópia no pacote completaram com os mesmos totais.

Evidência: [geometry-proof.json](portable-essentials/geometry-proof.json), [PAGES.json](portable-essentials/PAGES.json), [manifest.json](portable-essentials/manifest.json), [gerador rerodável](portable-essentials/prepare_portable_essentials.py).

## Source SHA search

Searched SHA-256 of every PNG in `Assets` and `ArtSources` against the 52 descriptor `sourceSha256` values (52 unique targets). The scan covered **1,041 Assets PNGs / 1.166 GB / 3.27 s** and **303 ArtSources PNGs / 519.7 MB / 1.89 s** on this machine; timings are approximate and cache state is unspecified.

| Location searched | Exact source hash hits | Finding |
|---|---:|---|
| `Assets/**/*.png` | 0 / 52 | No original source content found in published assets. |
| `ArtSources/**/*.png` | 22 / 52 | All 22 matches are the corresponding reviewed staging PNG itself; for those sheets `sourceSha256 == imageSha256`. |
| Unmatched | 30 / 52 | These distinct source hashes have no exact PNG match anywhere in the scanned checkout. |

All 52 descriptor `sourcePath` locations are absent. The 22 identical reviewed PNGs preserve source bytes for those hashes, but they do not restore original path/history; the remaining 30 upstream PNG byte streams are unavailable here.

## Hurt rectangle overlaps

The three geometric overlaps are only low-alpha edge fringes, not opaque character pixels. The importer still rejects any rectangle overlap, even when alpha is near zero.

| Sheet | Frame intersection | Nonzero alpha | Max alpha |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Fists Hurt N/NE/E/SE | 250×1 px | 48 px, all alpha 1/255 | 1/255 |
| Katana Hurt N/NE/E/SE | 284×1 px | 28 px, all alpha 1/255 | 1/255 |
| Motorcycle Hurt N/NE/E/SE, pair 1 | 330×3 px | 95 px at alpha 1 + 3 px at alpha 2 | 2/255 |
| Motorcycle Hurt N/NE/E/SE, pair 2 | 296×1 px | 56 px, all alpha 1/255 | 1/255 |

Exact correction used only in the output package: keep the reviewed original canvas byte-for-byte in an unused region of each derived Hurt atlas; copy each complete frame crop into disjoint packed rectangles and point the derived frame rects there. Crop width/height, RGBA bytes, PPU, and normalized pivots remain identical, so frame-local pixels and anchor placement are preserved. Derived atlas dimensions/hash and rect locations change; the entire original canvas, including out-of-rect pixels and faint alpha, remains intact in the derived image.

## Can the reviewed archive stand in as a technical source?

**Technically yes, with explicit provenance separation.** The packaged descriptors point `sourcePath` at exact reviewed-PNG copies under the portable package's mirrored `ArtSources/.../staging/technical-source/`, bind technical `sourceSha256` to those copies, and set `sourceSize` to their native dimensions. They preserve historical generator path/hash/size under `provenance.originalAuthoredSource`; `rawSourceAvailable:false` avoids claiming raw recovery. The importer DTO ignores those additional provenance fields. This makes a reproducible *derived import input*; it does not restore the 30 distinct missing upstream PNGs or establish their generator/edit history, and it does not convert static review into runtime approval. The three overlap gates pass in this derived package only.
